Spatial Hull Roughness and Ship Hydrodynamics

2024–

A single mean roughness value can conceal hydrodynamically important spatial variation. This work quantifies how different roughness distributions with the same area-averaged equivalent roughness alter resistance, boundary-layer development, and wake structure. Current work extends this framework toward full-scale powering prediction, including effective roughness descriptors and measurement-informed spatial distributions.

CFD Parameterisation for Added Resistance in Waves

2026–

Added-resistance predictions can vary substantially across CFD setups and participating institutions, with the disagreement becoming particularly pronounced at specific wavelength ratios. This project maps that sensitivity through a structured parameter study to identify the modelling choices that drive the variation and support more reproducible CFD practice for ITTC seakeeping procedures.

CFD–MBD Analysis of Gyroscopic Wave Energy Converters

2025–

The internal gyroscopic dynamics are solved outside the CFD model using a simplified Python-based MBD solver, while CFD resolves the external wave–body hydrodynamics. The two solvers exchange motion, loads, and internal dynamic states in real time, forming a partitioned CFD–MBD framework that retains high-fidelity external flow prediction without resolving the complete mechanical system inside CFD.

CFD–MBD Wave energy Gyroscopic dynamics
