Ongoing Research

Spatial Hull Roughness and Ship Hydrodynamics

2024–

The work investigates how spatially non-uniform hull roughness distributions influence resistance, boundary-layer development, and wake response under identical area-averaged equivalent roughness. Current extensions move from model-scale resistance analysis toward full-scale powering prediction, with attention to effective roughness descriptors and measurement-informed roughness distributions.
