Ongoing Research

Spatial Hull Roughness and Ship Hydrodynamics

2024–

The work investigates how spatially non-uniform hull roughness distributions influence resistance, boundary-layer development, and wake response under identical area-averaged equivalent roughness. Current extensions address model-to-full-scale scaling, effective roughness descriptors, Reynolds-number sensitivity, and measurement-informed distributions.
